## Date Evidence

The dates above come from the timestamps of the files in the original 2022 collection. They are preserved here as historical evidence rather than inferred from the date on which the repository was organized.

Some files were later copied or extracted during the organization of the collection, which changed their current filesystem timestamps. When the original 2022 date is known from the source material, the historical date is preserved instead.
